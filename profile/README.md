## How we build

Every project, ours included, runs the same four steps, from the first diagram to production and the years after.

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Understand** | We map the business before the screens: who does what, where the money and the data move, what breaks today. |
| **Design** | Architecture and interface together. The system is drawn before a line is written. |
| **Build** | Short cycles and working software every week, with feedback in the loop. |
| **Run** | Deployment, monitoring, backups and the changes a live business needs. |

The rules we hold ourselves to:

- **The system before the screen.** Data model and workflows come first. Every amount, rule and permission is decided
  in one place, so what a customer is told always matches what our team sees.
- **Arabic and English, side by side.** Built in from the start, right to left included, never a translation pass at
  the end. Both languages are checked for parity on every change.
- **Checked before it ships.** Every change goes through review, linting, type checks, automated tests and a smoke
  test of the built image before it reaches production.
- **Switched on when proven.** New features ship dark behind a flag and are turned on only after they are verified
  against real data.
- **Data we can always get back.** Our records are backed up every night, and each backup is restored and compared
  against the live data before it is kept.
- **Fast and private by default.** Server rendering, a strict performance budget, and no tracking cookies or
  third-party scripts unless a project needs them.
- **Plain language.** Decisions, risks and costs are written down in words a business owner reads once.
