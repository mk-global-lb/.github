<p align="center"><b>Shop from anywhere. We handle the rest.</b></p>

MK Global helps shoppers, resellers and businesses in Lebanon buy from the world's biggest online stores (SHEIN,
AliExpress, Amazon, Temu, Alibaba, Noon, Namshi and Trendyol) with no foreign account or card. We place the order,
consolidate packages, ship by air from the UAE and by land from Saudi Arabia, and deliver to the door.

This organization is where the software behind that service is built.

## How we build

- **One source of truth.** Every amount, rule and permission is decided in one place, the operations platform, so
  what a customer is told always matches what our team sees.
- **Arabic and English, side by side.** Every screen is built for both languages, right to left included.
- **Checked before it ships.** Every change runs through automated tests before it reaches production.
- **Data we can always get back.** Our records are backed up every night, and each backup is restored and compared
  against the live data before it is kept.
